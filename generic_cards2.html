Card 1: Focus on Understanding

🫂

An argument doesn't have to be a battle where one person wins and the other loses. Instead, approach the conversation with the goal of understanding the other person's perspective. By focusing on empathy, you can work together to find a resolution that benefits you both.

“What can we do to solve this?”

Card 2: Explain Your Why

📜

People are moved by stories, not just facts. To get your point across, share a personal story about why this issue matters to you. Explaining your perspective through narrative helps the other person understand where you're coming from and makes them more likely to listen and connect.

“This issue is important to me because…”

Card 3: Find Common Ground

🔗

When a conversation becomes tense, look for the things you both agree on. Finding commonalities—even small ones—can be the foundation that holds the conversation together. These shared values or goals can help you move forward together, even when you disagree on other points.

“We both want a fair outcome.”

Card 4: Take a Pause

⏸️

When a conversation gets heated, it's normal to feel an urge to fight, flee, or freeze. These reactions can cause you to say or do something you later regret. When you feel this happening, take a moment to pause and breathe. Regaining control of your emotions allows you to respond with purpose instead of reacting impulsively.

Breathe, count to three, then respond.

Card 5: End with Respect

🏆

You don't have to walk away from a disagreement in a way that damages your relationship. Even if you don't reach a full agreement, you can end the conversation with integrity by making one clear, respectful point. Ending with dignity is always better than leaving the relationship in pieces.

One clear point, said with respect.
